# Check for Data Breaches

**Purpose:** Find out whether your email or passwords appeared in a known leak.
**Requirements:** A browser.

## Steps

1. Go to `haveibeenpwned.com` and search your email addresses.
2. Subscribe to notifications so you are alerted about new breaches.
3. Use your password manager's breach report, or Google Password Manager's **Checkup**.

## If you were in a breach

1. Change that account's password and any account that reused it.
2. Turn on two-factor authentication.
3. Be careful with unexpected emails, texts, and calls. Attackers often use leaked details.
4. If financial details leaked, contact your bank.
