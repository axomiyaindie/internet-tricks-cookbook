# Two-Factor Authentication

**Purpose:** Protect accounts so a stolen password alone is not enough.
**Requirements:** The account's security settings and a phone or security key.

## Choose the strongest option available

1. **Passkeys or hardware security keys** are the strongest.
2. **Authenticator app** codes (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Aegis, 2FAS) are good.
3. **SMS codes** are better than nothing but can be intercepted.

## Steps

1. Start with your email account, then banking, then social media and shopping.
2. Open the account's **Security** settings and turn on two-step verification.
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
4. Save the backup codes offline, for example printed and stored safely.

## Rules

- Never share a verification code. Real support teams do not ask for it.
- Keep a backup of your authenticator app or a second device.
