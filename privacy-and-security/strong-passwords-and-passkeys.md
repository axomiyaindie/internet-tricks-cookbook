# Strong Passwords and Passkeys

**Purpose:** Build passwords you will not need to remember, and move to passkeys where possible.
**Requirements:** A password manager (Bitwarden, 1Password, KeePassXC, or a browser's built-in one).

## Rules of thumb

- Length matters more than complexity. Aim for 14 or more characters.
- Use a passphrase of four or more random words for the one password you must remember.
- Use a different password for every account.
- Let the password manager generate and store the rest.

## Passkeys

Passkeys sign you in with your device lock (fingerprint, face, or PIN) and cannot be phished.

1. Open the account's security settings and look for **Passkeys**.
2. Create one and confirm with your device.
3. Keep another sign-in method as a backup until you are comfortable.

## Clean up

Run your password manager's report to find reused or weak passwords and change the important ones first.
