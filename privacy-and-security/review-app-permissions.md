# Review App and Account Permissions

**Purpose:** Remove old apps and sites that can still access your accounts.
**Requirements:** An account on each service.
**Note:** Menu names change between versions.

| Service | Where to review |
|---|---|
| Google | `myaccount.google.com/security-checkup` and Security > Your connections to third-party apps and services |
| Facebook | Settings > Apps and websites |
| X | Settings and privacy > Security and account access > Apps and sessions |
| GitHub | Settings > Applications |
| Android | Settings > Security and privacy > Privacy > Permission manager |
| iPhone | Settings > Privacy and Security |

## Steps

1. Open each page and list apps you no longer use.
2. Remove access for anything unfamiliar or unused.
3. Check for apps with broad access, such as reading all email or files.
4. Repeat every few months.
