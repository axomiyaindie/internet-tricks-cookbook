# Free SMS Verifications

Purpose:      Bypass mandatory mobile phone verification walls on apps and websites without disclosing your personal phone number to data brokers.
Requirements: A modern web browser. No technical experience required.

---

## The Privacy Threat of Mobile Verification

Most online platforms now force users to enter a phone number during sign-up under the guise of security. However, your personal cell number acts as a permanent, universal digital fingerprint. 

Once a service links your number to an account, it is often:
*   Cross-referenced with marketing profiles to trace your identity across the web.
*   Leaked in corporate data breaches, leading to spam calls and phishing texts.
*   Used to target high-value SIM-swapping account hijacking attempts.

## Practical Solutions for Temporary SMS Codes

To bypass validation check walls without revealing your primary phone line, you can use layered virtual receiving alternatives.

### Method 1: Public "Receive SMS Online" Pools
There are open web registries that provide lists of public phone numbers from multiple countries. Anyone can use these numbers to view incoming verification codes on a public webpage.

*   **How to use:** Copy a listed number, enter it into the app's sign-up form, and refresh the website's text log stream to grab your temporary verification code.
*   **Vetted Services:** Tools like **Receive-SMS-Free.net**, **SMSReceiveFree.com**, and **AnonymSMS.com** host hundreds of active public routing lines.
*   **Limitation:** Because these numbers are fully public, they should **never** be used for banking, secure email setups, or any account that holds sensitive personal details. Other users can see the service texts.

### Method 2: Disposable VoIP Numbers (Higher Security)
If you need a private verification line that only you can access—without signing up for a expensive contract—use a throwaway virtual burner number.

*   **Vetted Apps:** Services like **TextNow**, **Hushed**, or **Burner** provide a private, cloud-hosted phone number inside an app sandbox.
*   **Cookbook Trick:** Many of these apps give you a free initial phone number or a low-cost trial. You can use this dedicated burner number to sign up for services, verify the account, and then instantly discard the number.

## Bypassing Strict "Anti-VoIP" Filters

Some strict services (like banking apps or major platforms) actively block virtual, temporary, or VoIP-based phone numbers. 

If your virtual burner number gets rejected with an "invalid phone number" error message:
1.  **Use a temporary prepaid SIM:** Buy a cheap, non-contract, pay-as-you-go cash SIM card from a local convenience store.
2.  **Verify the account:** Put the SIM into an old backup phone, clear the platform verification check wall, and then remove the card.
3.  **Discard or Store:** You keep your true primary cell identity entirely separate from that digital account signature.
