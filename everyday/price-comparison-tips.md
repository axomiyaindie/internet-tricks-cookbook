# Price Comparison Tips for Flights and Hotels

**Purpose:** Find better prices with legitimate methods.
**Requirements:** A browser.

## Flights

1. Use a price-comparison tool such as Google Flights, then check the airline's own site for the same fare.
2. Turn on flexible dates or the price graph to see cheaper days.
3. Set a price alert and let the tool email you drops.
4. Compare nearby airports and one-way vs round-trip pricing.

## Hotels

1. Compare the hotel's direct rate with comparison sites. Direct bookings often include perks or flexible cancellation.
2. Check the total with taxes and fees, not the headline nightly rate.
3. Look for free cancellation and book early, then rebook if the price drops.

## Currency and region

1. Compare the final price in your home currency, including your card's foreign transaction fee.
2. Some sites show different prices by country. Check the terms of the site and your card before changing your region. Using a VPN to change regional pricing can break a site's terms.

## Myths

- Incognito mode does not reliably lower prices. Price changes usually come from demand and inventory.
