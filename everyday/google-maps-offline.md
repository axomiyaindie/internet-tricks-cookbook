# Google Maps: Offline Maps and Location Sharing

**Purpose:** Use maps without internet and share your location safely.
**Requirements:** The Google Maps app.
**Note:** Menu names change between versions.

## Download an offline map

1. Open Google Maps and tap your profile picture.
2. Choose **Offline maps** > **Select your own map**.
3. Adjust the area and tap **Download**.
4. In the offline settings, choose Wi-Fi only downloads to save mobile data.

Offline maps update automatically on Wi-Fi and expire if they are not updated for a long time.

## Share your location

1. Tap the blue dot for your location > **Share location**.
2. Pick a duration and who can see it.
3. Stop sharing anytime from the same menu.

## Other tips

- Save places to **Lists** like Want to go or Favorites.
- Add multiple stops to a route with **Add stop**.
