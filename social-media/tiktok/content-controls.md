# TikTok: Content Controls

**Purpose:** Tune your For You feed and set limits on what and how long you watch.
**Requirements:** The TikTok app.
**Note:** Menu names change between versions.

## Tune the feed

- Press and hold a video > **Not interested** to see less like it.
- Settings and privacy > **Content preferences** > **Refresh your For You feed** to start fresh.
- Settings and privacy > **Content preferences** > **Filter video keywords** to hide videos with chosen words or hashtags.

## Safety and time

- **Restricted Mode** (Content preferences) filters content that may not suit all audiences.
- **Screen time** or **Digital Wellbeing** settings let you set a daily limit and break reminders.

## History

Settings and privacy > **Activity center** > **Watch history** to review or clear what you watched.
