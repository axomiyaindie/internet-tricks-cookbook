# Instagram: Control Your Feed

**Purpose:** Choose what Instagram shows you and reduce unwanted content.
**Requirements:** The Instagram app.
**Note:** Menu names change between app versions. Search the settings screen if you cannot find an item.

## Switch feeds

Tap the Instagram logo at the top of the home screen to switch between **For you**, **Following**, and **Favorites**.

## Favorites

1. Open your profile and tap **Following**.
2. Add accounts to **Favorites** so their posts appear higher in your feed.

## Mute instead of unfollowing

Profile > **Following** > pick an account > **Mute** to hide its posts or stories without unfollowing.

## Tune recommendations

- On a suggested post, tap the three dots > **Not interested**.
- Settings and activity > **Suggested content** > **Reset suggested content** to clear your recommendations.

## Limit time

Settings and activity > **Your activity** (or **Time management**) to see time spent and set a daily limit or quiet mode.
