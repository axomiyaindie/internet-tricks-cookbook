# Instagram: Privacy and Safety Settings

**Purpose:** Lock down who can see and contact you on Instagram.
**Requirements:** The Instagram app.
**Note:** Menu names change between versions.

## Settings to review

| Setting | Where to find it | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Private account | Settings and activity > Account privacy | Only approved followers see your posts |
| Close Friends | Profile menu > Close Friends | Share stories with a chosen list |
| Hidden Words | Settings and activity > Hidden words | Filters offensive comments and message requests |
| Restrict | Profile of the person > Restrict | Hides their comments and moves their messages to requests, without telling them |
| Limits | Settings and activity > Limits | Temporarily limits comments and messages from non-followers |
| Tag approval | Settings and activity > Tags and mentions | Lets you approve tags before they show on your profile |
| Activity status | Settings and activity > Messages and story replies | Hides when you were last active |

## Tips

- Turn on two-factor authentication under **Accounts Center > Password and security**.
- Review **Apps and websites** and remove ones you no longer use.
