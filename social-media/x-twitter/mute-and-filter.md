# X (Twitter): Mute, Filter, and Organize

**Purpose:** Clean up your timeline and notifications.
**Requirements:** An X account.
**Note:** Menu names change between versions.

## Mute words and accounts

1. Settings and privacy > **Privacy and safety** > **Mute and block** > **Muted words**.
2. Add words, phrases, or hashtags. Choose where to mute (home timeline, notifications) and for how long.
3. Mute an account from its profile menu to hide it without unfollowing.

## Filter notifications

Settings and privacy > **Notifications** > **Filters** to hide notifications from people you do not follow, new accounts, or low-quality accounts.

## Organize

- Create **Lists** to read a curated timeline of selected accounts.
- Use **Bookmarks** to save posts privately for later.
- Settings > **Accessibility, display, and languages** > **Data usage** to turn off video autoplay.
