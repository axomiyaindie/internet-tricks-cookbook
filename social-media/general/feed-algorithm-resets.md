# Reset Recommendation Feeds

**Purpose:** Clear stale or unwanted recommendations on major platforms.
**Requirements:** An account on the platform.
**Note:** Menus move around between app versions. Search the settings screen for the option name if you cannot find it.

## YouTube

1. Open your profile > Settings > Manage all history.
2. Delete watch history, or pause it so new videos stop influencing the feed.
3. On any recommended video, use the three-dot menu > Not interested or Don't recommend channel.

## Instagram

Settings and activity > Suggested content > Reset suggested content.

## TikTok

Settings and privacy > Content preferences > Refresh your For You feed.

## X (Twitter)

Use the three-dot menu on a post > Not interested in this post. Check Settings > Privacy and safety > Content you see.

## Facebook

Settings > Feed preferences. Use Snooze, Unfollow, or Favorites to tune what shows up.

## General tips

- Actively like and follow what you want. Platforms weight engagement heavily.
- Search for topics you want, then interact with a few results.
- Resetting is temporary if you keep engaging with the same content.
