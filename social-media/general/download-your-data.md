# Download Your Data From Social Platforms

**Purpose:** Export a copy of your account data before deleting an account or switching services.
**Requirements:** An account on each platform.
**Note:** Menu paths change. Requests can take hours or days.

| Platform | Where to request it |
|---|---|
| Google and YouTube | `takeout.google.com` |
| Instagram and Facebook | Accounts Center > Your information and permissions > Download your information |
| X | Settings and privacy > Your account > Download an archive of your data |
| TikTok | Settings and privacy > Account > Download your data |
| LinkedIn | Settings > Data privacy > Get a copy of your data |
| Reddit | Account settings, then the data request option |
| WhatsApp | Settings > Account > Request account info |

## Tips

- Export before you deactivate or delete an account.
- Download links usually expire, so save the file soon.
- Archives contain private information. Store them somewhere safe.
