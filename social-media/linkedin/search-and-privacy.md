# LinkedIn: Search and Privacy

**Purpose:** Search LinkedIn precisely and control who sees your activity.
**Requirements:** A LinkedIn account.
**Note:** Menu names change between versions.

## Boolean search

Use capital AND, OR, NOT, quotes, and parentheses in people and job searches:

```
"product manager" AND (fintech OR payments) NOT intern
```

## Job search

- Save a search and turn on **Job alert** to get new matches by email.
- Use **Open to Work** with the recruiter-only option if you do not want a public banner.

## Privacy

- Settings > **Visibility** > **Profile viewing options** to choose what others see when you view their profile.
- Settings > **Visibility** > **Connections** to decide who can see your connections.
- **Edit public profile and URL** to set a clean custom link.
- Settings > **Data privacy** > **Get a copy of your data** to download your data.
