# WhatsApp Privacy Settings

**Purpose:** Control who can see you and contact you on WhatsApp.
**Requirements:** The WhatsApp app.
**Note:** Menu names change between versions.

## Settings > Privacy

- **Last seen and online**, **Profile photo**, **About**: choose Everyone, My contacts, or Nobody.
- **Read receipts**: turn off blue ticks for one-to-one chats.
- **Groups**: choose who can add you to groups.
- **Calls** > **Silence unknown callers**.
- **Default message timer**: make new chats disappearing by default.
- **Privacy Checkup** walks you through the main options.

## Protect chats

- **Chat lock**: open a chat, tap the contact name > **Chat lock** to hide it behind your fingerprint or PIN.
- **View once**: send photos or videos that disappear after being opened.
- **Disappearing messages**: open a chat, tap the name > **Disappearing messages**.

## Protect your account

Settings > **Account** > **Two-step verification** and set a PIN. Never share the six-digit code WhatsApp sends you.
