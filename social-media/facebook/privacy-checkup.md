# Facebook: Privacy Checkup

**Purpose:** Review who sees your posts and what data Facebook and apps can use.
**Requirements:** A Facebook account.
**Note:** Menu names change between versions.

## Steps

1. Settings & privacy > **Privacy Checkup** and walk through each section.
2. Settings & privacy > **Settings** > **Privacy** and set **Who can see your future posts**.
3. Use **Limit past posts** to change old public posts to Friends only.
4. Open **Activity log** to review and remove old posts and likes.
5. Settings > **Apps and websites** and remove apps you do not use.
6. Accounts Center > **Password and security** to turn on login alerts and two-factor authentication.
7. Accounts Center > **Your information and permissions** to review activity from other apps and websites.

## Tips

- Repeat this check every few months.
- Check what a stranger sees with **View as** or by opening your profile in a private window.
