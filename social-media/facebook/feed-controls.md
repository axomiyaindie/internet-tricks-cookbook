# Facebook: Feed Controls

**Purpose:** Make Facebook show you people and posts you actually want.
**Requirements:** A Facebook account.
**Note:** Menu names change between versions.

## Use the feed options

1. Open **Feeds** (left menu on desktop) to switch between Home, Friends, Favorites, and Following.
2. Use **Favorites** to prioritize people and Pages you care about most.

## Control individual items

- Three dots on a post > **Hide post** or **Snooze** the person or Page for 30 days.
- **Unfollow** someone to stop seeing their posts but stay friends.
- Settings & privacy > **Feed** (feed preferences) to manage Favorites, snoozed, and unfollowed lists in one place.

## Reduce clutter

- Leave inactive groups and unfollow Pages you no longer read.
- Use **Not interested** on posts you do not want more of.
