# Firefox: Containers and Reader View

**Purpose:** Separate accounts and sites from each other, and read pages without clutter.
**Requirements:** Firefox.

## Multi-Account Containers

1. Install **Firefox Multi-Account Containers** from the official Mozilla add-ons site.
2. Create containers such as Work, Personal, and Shopping.
3. Open a tab in a container from the container button, or assign a site to always open in one.
4. Each container keeps its own cookies and logins, so you can sign in to two accounts at once.

Mozilla's **Facebook Container** extension isolates Facebook from the rest of your browsing in the same way.

## Reader View

- Click the page icon in the address bar, or press `F9`, to strip ads and clutter.
- Adjust fonts, width, and theme, or use read aloud.
- Not every page supports Reader View.
