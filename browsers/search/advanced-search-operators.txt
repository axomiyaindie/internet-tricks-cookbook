ADVANCED SEARCH OPERATORS
Purpose:      Get precise results from Google-style search engines.
Requirements: None. Works in most major search engines (Bing and DuckDuckGo
              support most of these; behavior can differ slightly).

EXACT AND EXCLUDE
"exact phrase"            Match the words in this exact order
-word                     Exclude results containing this word
word1 OR word2            Match either term
*                         Wildcard: "best * for beginners"

LIMIT TO A SITE OR TYPE
site:example.com          Search only one website
site:.edu                 Search only one domain type
filetype:pdf              Only PDF files (also docx, xlsx, pptx, csv, txt)
intitle:keyword           Keyword must appear in the page title
inurl:keyword             Keyword must appear in the URL
intext:keyword            Keyword must appear in the page body

RANGES AND DATES
100..500                  Numbers in a range: "laptop $100..$500"
after:2025-01-01          Results published after a date
before:2026-01-01         Results published before a date
AROUND(5)                 Two terms within 5 words: "solar AROUND(5) subsidy"

QUICK ANSWERS
define:word               Dictionary definition
10 km to miles            Unit conversion card

COMBINED EXAMPLES
site:gov.in filetype:pdf "annual report" after:2024-01-01
intitle:"index of" "lecture notes" -site:pinterest.com
"how to" "raspberry pi" site:reddit.com OR site:stackoverflow.com

TIP
Use Tools > Any time on the results page to filter by date without operators.
